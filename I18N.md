# Internationalization contract

## Supported at launch

- `en` — English
- `pt-br` — Português do Brasil
- `zh-hans` — 简体中文 / Simplified Chinese

Prepared, but not implemented at launch: `zh-hant` for Traditional Chinese.

## Routing

Every first-class locale has an explicit prefix:

- `/en/`
- `/pt-br/`
- `/zh-hans/`

`/` is the `x-default` language gateway. It does not silently geolocate or redirect users.

## Editorial rule

A blog post may exist in one, two, or all three languages. Missing translations are not auto-filled or silently replaced by another locale. Related translations share a `translationKey`.

## Translation quality

- `FormaSL` and `Forma Research Lab` are never translated as brand names.
- The current Chinese baseline is Simplified Chinese (`zh-Hans`).
- Technical Chinese copy should receive native-language review before a high-stakes launch or claim-heavy publication.
- Machine translation may be used as a drafting aid, not as publication authority.

## Typography

No font binaries are included in this repository baseline.

- Latin preference: Instrument Sans, then system sans fallbacks.
- Simplified Chinese preference: Noto Sans SC / Noto Sans CJK SC, then Source Han Sans SC / PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei.
- Code preference: Commit Mono, then system monospace fallbacks.

For mainland-China resilience, do not depend on Google Fonts or another third-party font CDN at runtime.
