The architecture becomes a business through three distinct elements.

**Forma is the company.** It develops Semantic Computing and builds the ecosystem around this architecture.

**FormaSL is the open-source language.** It defines semantic contracts independently from the technologies that realize them. An organization's meaning remains under the organization's control.

**Forma Platform is the commercial product.** It connects FormaSL contracts to data, databases, services, models, agents and infrastructure and provides the mechanisms to operate them in production.

Clouds provide primitives such as compute, storage, databases, queues and models. Forma adds a level of abstraction above them: developers begin with the concepts, relations and rules of the system and use the platform to realize them.

Traditional code and infrastructure remain composable with that layer.

**We do not sell the language. We sell the ability to put computable meaning into production.**
