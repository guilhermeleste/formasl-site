Once meaning and rules become explicit and preservable, computers can operate directly over them.

The same world can be analyzed by graph algorithms, feed statistical models, participate in reasoning, be explored through hypothetical scenarios and govern decisions and actions. The point is not to collect many capabilities in one language. It is to let them compose without rebuilding meaning at every transition.

A graph analysis can feed a machine-learning model. The model produces a prediction. The prediction participates in reasoning. The reasoning contributes to a decision. The decision authorizes an action. The effect returns as an observation.

Prediction, inference, decision, action and observation remain semantically distinct throughout the chain.

**The computational regime changes. Meaning remains continuous.**
