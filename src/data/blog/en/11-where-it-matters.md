Semantic Computing creates the most leverage where the same concepts and rules cross many systems and semantic disagreement has real consequences.

In **defense**, units, capabilities, space, time, intelligence, authority, plans and effects cross planning, logistics, command and operations. In **finance**, clients, assets, positions, contracts, risk, orders and regulation cross trading, compliance, credit, treasury and accounting.

In **industry and energy**, physical assets, processes, maintenance, production, networks and dependencies must keep identity across ERPs, sensors, engineering and operational systems. In **logistics**, ownership, responsibility, routes, contracts and events cross companies and jurisdictions.

In **healthcare**, observations, hypotheses, diagnoses, predictions and decisions cross professionals and institutions. In **government**, rights, competencies, authorities and decisions cross agencies.

The pattern is the same: organizations must control their domains, but they should not rebuild the generic infrastructure required to make those domains computable inside every application.

**The organization owns its world. Forma provides the infrastructure to make it work.**
