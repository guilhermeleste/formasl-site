Representing the world correctly at the input is not enough. Information crosses queries, models, inference, services, decisions and execution. Every transition can erase context required to understand the result.

Forma calls **Semantic Continuity** the property by which materially necessary distinctions remain available or reconstructible through those transformations.

A prediction remains a prediction after crossing a service. A result produced inside a scenario remains connected to the hypothesis that made it possible. A decision remains distinct from the execution it authorized. A claim remains attributable to its source.

Physical representation can change radically. A mechanism may use tables, indexes, graphs or tensors. Identity, context, time, authority, provenance and other necessary distinctions remain recoverable.

**Representation can change. Meaning must remain recoverable.**
